# WavuMall

**Shop local. Stay connected. Even offline.**

WavuMall is a marketplace that connects you with vendors across Kenya. Browse their products, build your cart, place orders, and shop with confidence — whether you're on WiFi, mobile data, or completely offline.

---

## What You Can Do

### 🛍 Browse the marketplace
Discover vendors near you and across the country. Search by name, explore their storefronts, and view their featured products — all at a glance.

### 🛒 Shop with ease
Add products to your cart, adjust quantities, and check out per vendor. Your cart remembers everything, even if you close the app.

### 📦 Track your orders
See your order history with clear status indicators for payment and delivery. Know exactly where each order stands.

### 📶 Shop offline
No signal? No problem. WavuMall works fully offline — browse vendors, add to cart, and place orders. Everything syncs to the marketplace when you're back online.

### 🔄 Stay in sync
- **Manual sync** — Tap the sync button whenever you want to push your orders and pull the latest updates.
- **Auto‑sync** — Turn it on in Settings and let the app keep you up to date automatically at your preferred interval.

### 🌙 Light & dark mode
Shop in the theme that suits you. Your preference is remembered.

### 🔐 Your account, your control
- Set a password to unlock the app — verified on your device, never sent anywhere just to open the app.
- A 4‑digit recovery PIN is available if you ever forget your password. It stays on your device, always.
- Update your details anytime from Settings.

---

## How It Works

1. **Create your profile** — On first launch, set up your name, phone number, email, and a password.
2. **Browse & shop** — Explore vendors, add items to your cart, and place orders.
3. **Sync when ready** — Push your orders to the marketplace and pull down any updates from vendors.
4. **Track everything** — Review your order history and watch your orders progress.

---

## Setting Up Sync

To connect to the marketplace:

1. Open the app and go to **Settings**.
2. Enter the marketplace server address provided to you.
3. Tap **Test** to confirm the connection.
4. Tap **Save**.

You only need to do this once. After that, sync happens with a single tap — or automatically if you turn on Auto‑Sync.

---

## Tips for the Best Experience

- **Turn on Auto‑Sync** if you want your orders to reach vendors without lifting a finger.
- **Use the recovery PIN** only if you forget your password — it's your safety net.
- **Pull down to refresh** on the vendor list to see the latest storefronts.
- **Group your cart by vendor** — WavuMall places one order per vendor, so you'll see separate checkouts for each.
